# Code of conduct

This project serves public-sector grant staff and the people who help them. We expect everyone who takes part (issues, pull requests, discussions) to:

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with the work, not the person.
- Keep feedback specific and useful: say what you tried and what happened.
- Not harass, demean or discriminate against anyone.
- Not post private information about others, including names, contact details or internal agency material.
- Not post secrets, credentials or anything you are not allowed to share.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or block participants who do not follow these rules. To report a conduct concern, use the private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). The repository owner is not yet named; a contact will be added here when one is chosen.
